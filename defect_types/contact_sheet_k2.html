These are not verified labels. They are the model's own grouping of its internal features. Before training anything on them, run label_check.py: it asks you to sort a blind sample by hand and reports how often you agree with the cluster.

High agreement means the grouping tracks something physically real. Agreement near chance means it does not, and no training accuracy would tell you otherwise, because a stage-two model is only reproducing the clustering it was trained on.
